About Kikandwa Community Secondary School

Who We Are

Kikandwa Community Secondary School is committed to creating a supportive environment where learners develop academically, socially and personally. Our guiding values are excellence, discipline and success.

Our Mission

To support learners with quality teaching, strong values and opportunities to grow into responsible citizens.

Our Vision

To nurture confident, skilled and principled learners prepared to contribute positively to their communities.
